Personal Analytics Project

HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

Microsoft Power BI | Power Query | DAX

Analysed employee attrition patterns across departments, gender, overtime, work-life balance, job satisfaction, and other workforce characteristics to identify factors associated with employee turnover.

Prepared and transformed the dataset using Power Query, developed DAX measures for key performance indicators, and built an interactive Power BI dashboard to support workforce analysis and decision-making.

Key Insights

  • Analysed 1,470 employee records.
  • 237 employees left the organisation, resulting in a 16% attrition rate.
  • Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate of 31% compared with 10% among employees who did not work overtime.
  • Sales had the highest departmental attrition rate at 21%.
  • Employees with poor work-life balance had an attrition rate of 31%.

BCG X – Introduction to Data for Decision Makers

Boston Consulting Group (BCG) | 2026

Completed a practical data analytics simulation focused on using campaign performance data to support business decision-making. The task required analysing campaign and channel performance, creating simple visualizations, identifying the strongest-performing combination, and communicating a clear recommendation to the client.

Business Question

Which campaign and channel combination should the client double down on to grow new-customer sales — and why?

What I Did

  • Analysed campaign performance across different marketing channels.
  • Compared total sales generated by Campaign A and Campaign B across Email, Instagram, and Web Banner channels.
  • Compared the number of new customers converted by each campaign and channel combination.
  • Created simple charts to communicate the strongest performing combinations.
  • Synthesised the analysis into a client-focused recommendation.
  • Presented the findings through an executive-ready slide.

Key Finding

Campaign A + Email was the strongest combination in the analysis, generating $2,124 in total sales and 29 converted customers.

Recommendation

Prioritize Campaign A through the Email channel to grow new-customer sales, as this combination produced the strongest results across both total sales and converted customers.
