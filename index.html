About Me

A data professional combining monitoring and evaluation, data management, reporting, and analytics.

I am a data professional with experience in monitoring and evaluation, data management, data quality assurance, reporting, and data analysis. My professional experience involves reviewing and validating programme data, maintaining databases, monitoring data quality, preparing reports, and providing reliable data for donor and management decision-making.

In my current role, I analyse programme data for internal and external reporting, conduct data quality reviews, maintain programme databases, and support monitoring activities that assess project outcomes and impact.

Alongside my professional experience, I am developing my data analytics and business intelligence skills through hands-on projects. I have built dashboards using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables, and Google Data Studio/Looker Studio, including projects in healthcare, sales, wildlife operations, and pangolin rehabilitation monitoring.

My goal is to combine my experience in data management and monitoring and evaluation with strong analytical and technical skills to solve real-world problems and support evidence-based decision-making.
